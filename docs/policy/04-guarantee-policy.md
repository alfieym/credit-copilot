# Guarantee Policy

## Guarantor Eligibility

- The guarantor must be the group's parent company or an equally qualified entity already credited by the bank.
- Third-party guarantors must provide audited financial statements for the past two years and meet minimum capital requirements.

## Unsecured Exposure

- Unsecured trade finance must post a margin of no less than 10% of the exposure amount.
- Total clean (unsecured) exposure must not exceed 30% of the group limit.
