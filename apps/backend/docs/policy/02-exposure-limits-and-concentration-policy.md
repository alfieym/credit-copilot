# Exposure Limits and Concentration Policy

## Single Group Limit

- A single group's consolidated exposure must not exceed its approved consolidated exposure limit; if exceeded, it must be reduced or collateralized within 30 days.

## Concentration Warning

- When a group's consolidated exposure utilization (consolidated exposure / limit) reaches 80%, a warning is triggered; at 100%, new credit is suspended.

## Single Borrower Limit

- A single borrower's exposure must not exceed 15% of the bank's net tier-1 capital.
