# Rating Access Policy

## Rating Threshold

- Borrowers with an internal rating of BB- or below (BB-, B+, B, B-, CCC+, CCC, CCC-, CC, C, D) are prohibited from new credit; existing exposure is maintained with a reduction plan.

## Outlook Management

- Borrowers with a Negative outlook require strengthened post-lending monitoring, from quarterly to monthly.
- Borrowers with a Developing outlook have new credit suspended until the rating is clarified.

## External Rating Reference

- If an S&P / Moody's external rating is more than two notches below the internal rating, a rating-difference review is triggered.
