# Industry Access Policy

## Real Estate Access

- New credit to the real estate industry must be approved by the head-office risk committee.
- A real-estate borrower group's consolidated exposure must not exceed 70% of its consolidated exposure limit.
- New exposure for land-reserve and commercial-property development project finance is generally not granted.

## Restricted Industries (High Consumption / Overcapacity)

- Steel, cement, electrolytic aluminum, flat glass, and other high-consumption/overcapacity industries only maintain existing exposure; no new exposure.

## Technology Industry Support

- Semiconductors, data centers, software services, and other technology industries are on the key-support list; their exposure limits may be raised by 10%.
